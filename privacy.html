Privacy Policy

Project draft — final legal review required before publication.

This draft describes the behavior of the supplied website build. D&R Realty should have counsel review it before publication.

Information you provide

The preview contact form validates information in your browser and opens your email application addressed to D&R Realty. The supplied static build does not itself store the form submission on a server.

Analytics and attribution

The build preserves campaign parameters such as UTM values in browser session storage so they can be passed to a future CRM integration. Google Analytics is not activated until D&R Realty supplies an approved measurement ID and consent configuration.

Third-party services

The website may load Google Maps, Pexels media and images hosted on D&R Realty’s current website. Those providers may process technical information under their own policies.
